Who's behind it

About Mannan

I design YouTube thumbnails for creators across Pakistan and beyond — vloggers, challenge channels, tech reviewers and creators teaching others how to grow. Every thumbnail is built around one question: will this win the thumbnail next to it?

That means bold type that reads at 120px wide, faces and props that carry emotion, and color choices that hold up against YouTube's white UI. If it doesn't survive the scroll, it doesn't ship.
